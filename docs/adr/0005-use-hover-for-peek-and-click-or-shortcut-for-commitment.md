# Use Hover for Peek and Click or Shortcut for Commitment

Hovering over the Island should produce a lightweight Peek, while click and global shortcut are the primary ways to commit into the Expanded Island. Dragging files into the Island enters Drag Target immediately because the user's intent is already explicit.

This preserves the feeling of speed without making the Island feel jumpy or intrusive. A UI/UX-first product should reward curiosity on hover, but avoid stealing attention or focus until the user makes a stronger gesture.
